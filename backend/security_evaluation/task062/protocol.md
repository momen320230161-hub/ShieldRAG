## Design fixed before scoring

A and B are byte-identical copies of the Task 05.1 and 06.1 models with their original policies. C uses one balanced linear SVM, C=0.5, seed 1729, equal sample weights, unchanged word/character TF-IDF and one shared operating point. This removes B's down-weighting of academic examples and its stage thresholds selected on small control counts. Use all previous training families and additional authored academic triplets plus inert policy-violation training controls. Never fit a vocabulary on validation. Use the previous development validation split plus new calibration families to select two global cuts. Review must withhold every calibration positive with rules enabled. Among qualifying review cuts maximize benign availability. Choose block above review by macro F1. Refuse deployment if confirmation fails. No hyperparameter search or language-specific exceptions.

Fresh qualification consists of complete question/passage/span groups and separate stage-specific negative controls. Groups, forms of simulated policy violations and subject matter are assigned before fitting. An output span may occur within its own group's context by design. Inspect normalized duplicates and near-duplicate texts across splits. Use whole-case availability (all three stages must pass) to rank, then fewer benign stage refusals, then lower operational complexity (incumbent first). Eligible challengers must retain every fresh malicious control, the previously calibrated development attacks, and all fresh Arabic and mixed-language whole-case successes of the incumbent in aggregate per language. To promote, require a strict gain in complete benign answers over A. Report individual errors even for withheld candidates. The selection program does not read historical RAG cases or Task 05 test results. Store dataset digests, candidate digests, cuts, rows and the decision in the lock before running historical replay. Do not adjust C in response to qualification, historical outcomes or regression tests. A failed release gate retains A on the production baseline.

Comparisons are developer-authored, offline and scripted. Frozen historical generator responses and separate schema-aware development results are reported separately. A local final-suite failure or a lost known security invariant vetoes release irrespective of classifier ranking. No live calls are authorized.
